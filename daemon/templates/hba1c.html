Custom GMI Parameters

GMI is a population estimate: people with the same average glucose can have different laboratory HbA1c results because of individual differences in red-cell lifespan and glycation.

Best Fit uses the laboratory HbA1c results in the date range currently displayed, paired with the 90-day average glucose available for each result. It does not use your entire history unless that is the range you are viewing. You can also enter your own constants and select Apply to update the chart.

HbA1c results must be entered in the Diary to be included here.

Standard GMI
GMI (%) = 3.31 + 0.4306 × mean glucose (mmol/L)

Standard eHbA1c
eHbA1c (%) = (mean glucose (mmol/L) + 2.59) ÷ 1.59

Your Custom GMI
HbA1c (%) = A + B × 90-day mean glucose (mmol/L)
