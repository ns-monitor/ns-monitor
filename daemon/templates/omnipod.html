Milestone significance
One-sample t-test of BG delta against zero, at 4 pre-chosen hours (not scanned across every bucket, to avoid a multiple-comparisons false-positive problem).
Hour Mean Δ (mmol/L) 95% CI n p-value Result
  • Window: -24h to +24h relative to each pod's own start (0 = that pod's own first BG reading at/after its start time).
  • A pod is only included once 24 hours of real time have passed since its start, so a full +24h forward window can exist.
  • Forward data is cut off at the next pod's own start if that happened sooner than +24h, so a short-lived pod's window never absorbs the next pod's own onset transition.
  • A pod is excluded entirely (not just truncated) if the pod immediately before it started less than 24 hours earlier -- otherwise this pod's -24h lookback would reach into that previous pod's own still-settling onset transition rather than its steady-state tail.
  • Pod counts: – eligible, – clean, – excluded.

BG Rate of Change Either Side of Pod Start

Same eligible/clean pod population and same -24h to +24h window as the trend chart above, but plotting the first derivative of BG (mmol/L per hour) instead of the delta from the pod's own start reading. Where the trend chart shows where BG sits relative to pod start, this shows how fast it's moving at each point -- a flat but elevated plateau and a BG still actively climbing look identical on the delta chart above, but are clearly distinguished here. Blue band is the 10th–90th percentile spread between pods at each point; orange band is the 95% confidence interval on the mean itself.
