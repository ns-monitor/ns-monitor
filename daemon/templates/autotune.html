Autotune Overview

What is Autotune?

Autotune is an automated physiological parameter tuning algorithm derived from AndroidAPS and oref0. It evaluates observational closed-loop data to tune 24 hourly basal rates, one scalar Insulin Sensitivity Factor (ISF), and one scalar Carb Ratio (IC).

Clinical Safety Boundary:

This is an analysis and reporting tool only. It never automatically overwrites pump profiles or modifies active insulin delivery. All recommendations must be reviewed against baseline settings and clinical context before enacting changes.

Sequential 04:00 Windowing:

Autotune does not pool data into an arbitrary multi-day average. It evaluates days sequentially from 04:00 to 04:00 local time, carrying forward tuned results into each subsequent day while strictly enforcing safety caps against your immutable baseline pump profile.

Why 04:00 to 04:00?

Midnight-to-midnight windows split nocturnal fasting periods in half. Autotune anchors each tuning day at 04:00 local time, capturing overnight sleep and basal equilibrium intact.

Excluding Days:

You can toggle individual days ON or OFF within the selected evaluation period. Consider deselecting days characterized by:

  • Acute illness, fever, or infection
  • Infusion site failures or occlusions
  • Unannounced meals or missing carb entries
  • Severe sensor dropouts or compression lows
Presets:

Clean Data Only: Automatically deselects days with material CGM gaps (>15 min) or <80% sensor coverage.

Autotune runs chronologically from oldest to newest day. Uncheck anomalous days to exclude them from parameter tuning:

Basal Curve & 3-Hour Lookback

How Basal Rates Are Tuned:

Autotune sums glucose deviations in basal periods for each clock hour. Rather than adjusting the current hour, it applies adjustments to the preceding 3 hours.

Why Preceding Hours?

Subcutaneous insulin has an absorption and peak depot delay. Glucose deviations observed at 06:00 are caused by basal insulin infused between 03:00 and 05:00.

Evidence Classifications

Four Classification Categories:
  • Meal / CSF: Active Carbs on Board (COB) or positive post-meal deviation tail. Used for Carb Ratio tuning.
  • UAM: Unannounced Meals / large excursions. Separated from clean fasting basal data.
  • Basal: Clean background periods without active meals or UAM where glucose change is attributable to basal rate.
  • ISF: Stable non-meal periods with active bolus or correction IOB. Used to evaluate insulin sensitivity.
